Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what data this application (the "Service") handles when it connects to third-party platforms such as TikTok to publish content on an account the Operator controls.

1. Who Operates the Service

The Service is a personal, single-operator automation project. It is not offered publicly, has no public user accounts, and does not collect data from any member of the public.

2. Data the Service Handles

DataPurpose
OAuth access and refresh tokensAuthenticate to the connected platform's API so the Service can publish on the Operator's own account.
Video files generated by the ServiceUploaded directly to the connected platform; not shared elsewhere.

The Service does not collect data about end viewers of the published content, does not use cookies or tracking pixels, and does not operate a website that visitors submit information to.

3. Storage and Security

Access tokens are stored in the Operator's own private infrastructure (private source control secrets and short-lived runtime caches) and are never written to public storage. Tokens are used only to call the connected platform's official API.

4. Sharing

No data is sold or shared with any third party. Tokens are used solely to communicate with the platform that issued them (e.g., TikTok's own API), as required for the Service to function.

5. Retention

Tokens are retained only as long as the Service continues to operate and are replaced automatically when the connected platform rotates them. Revoking the app's access from the platform's own account settings immediately invalidates stored tokens.

6. Your Controls

Because the Service only ever acts on the Operator's own connected account, access can be revoked at any time from that platform's app-permissions settings, which immediately stops the Service from publishing further content.

7. Changes

This Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued operation of the Service after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this Policy can be directed to the Operator through the platform account associated with the Service.
